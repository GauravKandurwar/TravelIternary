🗼 Eiffel Tower & Champ de Mars

▾

Route: Airbnb → Trocadéro → Eiffel Tower → Champ de Mars. Seeing the tower on your very first evening is absolutely worth it.

From home: walk about 15 minutes to Champ de Mars/Eiffel Tower. For Trocadéro first, take Metro 8 from École Militaire to La Motte-Picquet–Grenelle, then Metro 6 to Trocadéro.

✅ Explore

  • ✔Eiffel Tower up close
  • ✔Champ de Mars lawn
  • ✔Seine riverbank
  • ✔Family photos
  • ✔Let Nyra walk/play on the lawn

📸 Best experience

Stay until sunset to see: Daylight → Sunset → Illuminated tower ✨. The tower sparkles for 5 minutes on the hour after dark.

📖 A little history

Built by Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World's Fair, the 330 m iron tower was meant to be temporary and was widely criticised at the time. It survived because it made a perfect radio antenna — and became the symbol of Paris.

🌉 Pont Alexandre III

▾

Spend ~30–45 minutes at Paris's most ornate bridge.

✅ Look for

  • ✔Golden winged statues
  • ✔Ornate Art Nouveau lamps
  • ✔Seine & Eiffel Tower views
  • ✔Family photos
⭐ Best time: sunset / blue hour, as the lamps switch on.

📖 A little history

Named after Tsar Alexander III, the bridge was built for the 1900 World's Fair to symbolise the Franco-Russian alliance. Its low, single-span design was engineered so it wouldn't block the view of the Champs-Élysées and Les Invalides.

🧭 Getting Around Paris

🎫 Navigo Week

For both adults, buy Navigo Week zones 1–5 (currently €32.40 each). It runs Monday–Sunday, covers Sep 2–6 and includes Metro, bus, tram, RER and eligible CDG travel. Each traveler needs a separate pass/card or compatible phone.

👧 Nyra's fare

Children aged 4 to under 10 receive 50% off individual Metro–Train–RER and Bus–Tram tickets; age 4 is not automatically free. Reduced individual tickets will likely cost less than a full weekly pass for her.

📱 Apps

Citymapper and Bonjour RATP give live Metro routes. Download Google Maps offline for Paris.

👛 Watch for

Keep bags zipped on the Metro and near tourist spots — pickpockets target crowds around the Eiffel Tower and Louvre.

🚉 Your useful stops

École Militaire is your nearest Metro 8 station. Use Bosquet–Grenelle for Bus 92 and Bosquet–Saint-Dominique for Bus 69.

🚶 Validate every trip

Tap the pass when boarding buses/trams and at Metro/RER gates, including transfers. Keep it until the journey ends.

🌱 Vegetarian Ordering

Say this

Nous sommes végétariens. Sans viande, sans poisson et sans œuf, s'il vous plaît.
We are vegetarian. No meat, fish or egg, please.

Check hidden ingredients

Ask about meat/fish stock, egg in fresh pasta and pastries, and animal rennet in cheese. “Végétarien” in France may still include egg and dairy.

Cuisine mix

French bakery/crêperie breakfasts, Mexican at Tío, Italian at Chez Pippo and Capucci, plus Indian at Kamal Shri and the pure-vegetarian Arya Bhavan.

Reservations

Reserve Chez Pippo, Tío and Capucci where possible. Recheck opening hours shortly before the September 2026 trip.

💡 Family Tips

💧 Water

Carry a refillable bottle — Paris has free Wallace fountains with drinkable water.

🚻 Breaks

Plan a mid-afternoon rest at the Airbnb each day. It keeps Nyra (and everyone) happy.

🍦 Snacks

Boulangeries everywhere: croissants, crêpes, and ice cream are easy Nyra-friendly wins.

🎟️ Book ahead

Reserve the Louvre and Seine cruise online in advance to skip long queues.

▾

📖 A little background

Bachalpsee is a pair of small alpine lakes at 2,265 m. On clear, still days they reflect the Schreckhorn and surrounding peaks.

September comfort

Bring hats, sunscreen and refillable bottles. Rome can remain hot, and many ancient sites have limited shade.
